Five rules

Remember these and you avoid most scams.

1
Your UPI PIN is only for sending money.

You never enter it to receive money, claim a refund or win a prize.

2
Never share an OTP, PIN or CVV.

Not with a caller, a “bank officer”, or anyone who says they are helping you.

3
A QR scan or “collect request” sends money out.

Scanning a code or approving a request is for paying, never for receiving.

4
Don’t install screen-sharing apps or open unknown links.

AnyDesk, TeamViewer and APK files sent in messages give strangers control of your phone.

5
Cheated? Call 1930 at once.

Report at cybercrime.gov.in and tell your bank. The sooner, the better.

Not sure? Check before you pay.

It takes a few seconds, and it never needs your PIN or OTP.
